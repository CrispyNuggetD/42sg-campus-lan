01 / Launch

Your next terminal tab.

zsh · setup
{{quickstart}}

Git, tmux, Python 3.9+ with curses; terminal ≥ 76 × 28. Setup backs up and appends to .zshrc.

Then open a new zsh terminal: lan42
Or launch directly: ./lan42.sh

On Ubuntu, LAN42 opens in a second terminal tab with its own tmux session.

02 / Choose your lane

You’re welcome
as a guest.

Guest

Chat and games work without the API host. /join connects you to a friend.

Signed in

Campus seats need an approved host running on campus (currently hnah’s), configured trust and verified SG sign-in. Click Sign in, press F2 or use /signin, then press F7 or use /clusters for both clusters. /c1 and /c2 show one. Map colors distinguish friends, list members, signed-in users and guests.

Cloning alone does not provide the campus API service.

Share by choice.

/koel flock offers the campus group when its host is available. The host names additions like report_dthoo_1.pdf; edits and renames add copies, never replace or delete shared files. Upload and receive consent stay separate. File-sharing guide →

Shared uploads: 6 additions/minute; your own folder: 30/minute. Flock files live in ~/KoelDrop/42-singapore/YYYY-MM-DD/.

Ubuntu startup separately offers a dock launcher and a Files sidebar bookmark. Ask again with /koel dock or /koel bookmark.

Keep it comfortable.

/uwu asks before sound; exam quiet silences LAN42 audio. /notify controls popups; /notify all off disables them. Your preferences →
