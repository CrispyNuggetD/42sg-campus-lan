Installation help →
02 · Know your mode

Guest first. Sign in for campus tools.

Guest chat and games work without the API host. Try /who, /join to connect to a friend, or /games.

Campus seats need an approved host running on campus (currently hnah’s), configured host trust and verified SG sign-in. Click Sign in, press F2 or use /signin; then try /clusters. Pulling the repo alone does not provide this service.

Host offline or sign-in unavailable? →
03 · Make yourself comfortable

Share by choice. Keep it quiet.

/koel flock offers the campus file-sharing group while its host is available. Join/upload consent and incoming-file approval are separate; leaving keeps local copies.

/uwu introduces the bird courier and asks before sound. Exam quiet silences LAN42 audio. /notify controls popups; /notify all off disables them. /help opens the full reference; /quit exits.
